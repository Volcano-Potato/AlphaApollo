## When to use
When solving combinatorial problems with multiple conditions

## Strategy
- Enumerate all valid move combinations systematically.  
- Track intermediate results to avoid double-counting or missing cases.  
- Verify edge cases and ensure all constraints are satisfied.

## Avoid
Assuming a single path type or neglecting step size variations.
