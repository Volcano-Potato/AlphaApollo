## When to use
When solving a problem with multiple conditions or constraints

## Strategy
- Check domain validity of expressions before finalizing answers.  
- Validate intermediate steps with numerical examples.  
- Ensure all logarithmic and algebraic properties are correctly applied.

## Avoid
Assuming intermediate results are valid without verification.
