## When to use
When solving systems with multiple interdependent equations.

## Strategy
- Avoid symmetry assumptions without verification.  
- Check consistency across all equations.  
- Use substitution to reveal hidden constraints.

## Avoid
Assuming equal variables without validating against all equations.
