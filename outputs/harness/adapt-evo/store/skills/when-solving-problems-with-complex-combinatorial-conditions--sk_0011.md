## When to use
When encountering nested binomial coefficients or complex summations.

## Strategy
- Derive closed-form expressions for nested binomial terms.  
- Simplify before computing to reduce complexity.  
- Use modular arithmetic at each step to handle large numbers.

## Avoid
Leaving summations unresolved or providing incomplete answers.
