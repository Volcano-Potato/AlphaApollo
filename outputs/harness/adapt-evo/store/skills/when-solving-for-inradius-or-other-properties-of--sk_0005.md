## When to use
When calculating area of an equiangular hexagon with alternating side lengths.

## Strategy
- Use $ \frac{1}{2}ab\sin(\theta) $ for triangles with two sides and included angle.  
- Recognize consistent internal angles in equiangular polygons.  
- Multiply individual triangle areas by number of triangles.

## Avoid
Assuming all triangles have same side lengths or angles without verification.
