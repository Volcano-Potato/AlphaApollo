## When to use
When multiple constraints affect the validity of an arrangement.

## Strategy
- Systematically check all combinations under constraints.  
- Validate each condition explicitly.  
- Avoid assumptions; verify all required properties.

## Avoid
Failing to ensure all conditions are met in combinatorial problems.
