## When to use
When tangency and triangle formation are involved with potential symmetry.

## Strategy
- Verify geometric assumptions with diagrams.  
- Check if triangle properties align with given conditions.  
- Avoid assuming symmetry without proof.

## Avoid
Assuming a triangle is equilateral based on tangent properties without justification.
