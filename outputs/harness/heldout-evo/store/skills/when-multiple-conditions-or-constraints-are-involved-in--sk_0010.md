## When to use
When multiple constraints affect possible outcomes in a probability problem.

## Strategy
- Enumerate all valid configurations respecting constraints.  
- Calculate probabilities for each case systematically.  
- Sum probabilities weighted by their occurrence.

## Avoid
Overlooking distinct cases due to symmetry or constraint overlap.
